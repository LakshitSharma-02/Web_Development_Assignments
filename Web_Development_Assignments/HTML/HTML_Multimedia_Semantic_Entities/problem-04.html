Course Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to :

  • Read and analyze live market charts using price action and volume analysis.
  • Formulate robust trading strategies across Stocks, Options, Forex, and Crypto markets.
  • Calculate position sizing, manage risk-to-reward ratios, and protect capital during drawdowns.
  • Master trading psychology to eliminate emotional discipline failures in live trading.
