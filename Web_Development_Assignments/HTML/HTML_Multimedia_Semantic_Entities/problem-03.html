Destination Overview

Welcome to Paris

Paris, France's capital, is a major European city and a global center for art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. Its 19th-century cityscape is crisscrossed by wide boulevards and the River Seine.

One-Day Paris Highlights Itinerary

  1. 08:30 AM: Start early at the Louvre Museum to view the Mona Lisa.
  2. 12:00 PM: Stroll through Tuileries Garden & enjoy lunch at a French bistro.
  3. 02:30 PM: Climb to the top of Arc de Triomphe and walk down Champs-Élysées.
  4. 05:30 PM: Head to Trocadéro Plaza for stunning views and ascent up the Eiffel Tower.
  5. 08:30 PM: Relax on a Seine River night cruise under sparkling illuminations.
