Recruitment Stages

  1. National Qualifier Test (TCS NQT) / Profile Screening
  2. Technical Assessment & Interview
  3. Managerial Evaluation Round
  4. HR Round & Final Offer Generation
Screening
Eligibility check via the TCS NextStep Portal based on academic consistency (minimum 60% aggregate) and profile submission.
Technical Round
Hands-on coding evaluation, core CS fundamentals (DSA, DBMS, SQL, OS), and project deep-dives tailored to Ninja, Digital, or Prime tracks.
HR Round
Verification of academic documentation, behavioral assessment, shift/relocation flexibility, and alignment with Tata core values.
Offer
Formal issuance of the offer letter via NextStep, detailing joining track, location allocation, and training schedules.
