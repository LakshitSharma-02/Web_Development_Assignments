Admissions & Guidelines

Key Details

Eligibility
Candidate must fulfill academic aggregate criteria and qualify through GLAET / valid entrance exam score.
Scholarship
Special NCR region merit scholarships and fee waivers for top percentile scorers in board and national entrance tests.
Merit List
Evaluated on the basis of GLAET performance, academic record, and personal interview rounds.
Application Fee
A non-refundable registration fee of ₹700 payable online.

Greater Noida Campus Admission Steps

  1. Complete the online application form and select Greater Noida campus.
  2. Book the slot and clear the GLAET online assessment test.
  3. Attend the online/offline counselling session.
  4. Verify documents and deposit administrative fee to reserve the seat.
