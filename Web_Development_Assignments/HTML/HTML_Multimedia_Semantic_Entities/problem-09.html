Tournament Terminology & Formats

Match
A contest between two athletes or teams competing under specific sporting rules.
Fixture
A scheduled match arranged for a specific date, time, and arena venue.
League
A round-robin tournament structure where each participant plays against every other competitor.
Knockout
A single-elimination tournament format where the loser of each fixture is immediately eliminated.
