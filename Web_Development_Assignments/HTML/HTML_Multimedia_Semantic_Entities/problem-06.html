Restaurant Story

The Heritage of Rangrez

At Rangrez, we paint a culinary canvas that celebrates the vibrant tapestry of Indian heritage cooking. Established in 2012, our kitchen infuses age-old slow-cooking techniques with hand-pounded spices to bring royal Mughlai and North Indian delicacies directly to your table …

“Good food is like art — it speaks directly to the soul through rich aromas and vivid flavors.” — Rangrez Culinary Philosophy

Chef Profile

Master Chef Ranveer Chauhan

With over 18 years of experience across luxury heritage hotels in Jaipur and Lucknow, Chef Ranveer brings unmatched expertise in Dum Pukht cooking, artisan tandoori bread-making, and rich gravy reductions.

How to Place Your Order

  1. Step 1: Select your desired dishes from our online menu matrix.

  3. Step 3: Choose your order mode (Dine-in, Takeaway, or Express Delivery).
