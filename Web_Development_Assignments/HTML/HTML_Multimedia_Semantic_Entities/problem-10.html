Key Industry Terminology

Career Expo
An interactive event connecting students and professionals with top tier hiring teams, industry mentors, and business units.
Internship
A structured learning program offering hands-on project exposure for early-career talents ± stipend coverage.
Placement
Direct recruitment pathways into full-time analyst, associate, or consultancy roles within business divisions.
Workshop
An intensive, hands-on skill development track led by senior Deloitte subject matter experts.
Certification
Formal recognition awarded upon successfully mastering domain competencies and technical assessments.
Networking
Building strategic professional relationships with industry leaders, alumni, and peer innovators.

Attendee Journey Map

Follow these sequential steps to maximize your outcomes during the expo:

  1. Register on the portal and receive your unique Deloitte QR Pass.
  2. Attend the Keynote opening session in the Main Auditorium.
  3. Participate in domain-specific technical workshops and live coding sessions.
  4. Network with Deloitte hiring managers at regional business booths.
  5. Submit your application profile for priority internship & placement interviews.

Preparation Tips for Candidates

Ensure you are fully prepared before engaging with our global hiring leaders:

  • Update your resume to highlight measurable achievements, tech stacks, and project links.
  • Research Deloitte's primary practice arms: Risk Advisory, Tax, Audit, and Tech Consulting.
  • Prepare a 60-second elevator pitch summarizing your core competencies and career aspirations.
  • Review fundamentals on Cloud Systems, GenAI applications, and Enterprise Architecture.
  • Dress in professional business attire for virtual and on-site interviews.
